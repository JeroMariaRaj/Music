Rhythm of Tabla

Master the intricate rhythms and strokes of traditional Indian percussion.

Curriculum

  • Module 1: Basic Bols - Introduction to primary strokes (Na, Ta, Tin, Dhin).
  • Module 2: Talas - Playing basic rhythmic cycles like Teental and Keherwa.
  • Module 3: Compositions - Learning simple Kaydas and Tihais.
