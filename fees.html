Billing FAQs

Are there any hidden registration fees?
No! The prices you see above are all-inclusive. We do not charge any one-time registration fees or hidden administration costs.
Can I upgrade my plan later?
Absolutely. You can upgrade from Starter to Standard or Premium at any time. Your tuition will be pro-rated based on the days remaining in your current billing cycle.
Do I need to buy my own instrument?
While we provide instruments for use during your lessons at the academy, we highly recommend having an instrument at home for practice. We also offer affordable monthly instrument rentals for enrolled students.
