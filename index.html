Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need my own instrument?
While we provide instruments during lessons at the academy, we highly recommend having an instrument at home for daily practice to ensure steady progress. We offer instrument rental programs for beginners.
What age do you accept students?
We offer Early Childhood Music programs starting at age 4. Private instrumental lessons typically start around age 6-7, depending on the instrument and the child's readiness. We also have specialized adult programs!
How long are the lessons?
Standard private lessons are 30, 45, or 60 minutes long, depending on the student's age, level, and goals. Group classes typically run for 45 to 60 minutes.
