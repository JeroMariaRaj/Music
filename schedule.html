Important Guidelines

  • Arrival Time: Please arrive at least 10 minutes before your scheduled class time to warm up and tune your instruments.
  • Cancellations: We require a 24-hour notice for any lesson cancellations. Late cancellations will be charged as a full lesson.
  • Make-up Classes: Eligible for students who cancel 24 hours in advance. Must be scheduled within the same calendar month.
  • Instrument Rentals: Available at the front desk. Please request your rental at least a day prior to your scheduled class.

Need a custom time?

Can't find a slot that fits your schedule? We offer flexible private lessons outside of regular academy hours.
